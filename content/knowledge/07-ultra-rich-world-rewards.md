"Ode to Your Ultra Rich Taste" — get ready to win Ultra Rich prizes with every purchase.

official "Warp Oxblood Accordion Bag" — described as a 1-of-1 piece for Ultra Rich

## How to Participate

1. Purchase Ultra Rich products.
2. Earn entries based on your shopping value.
3. Enter the monthly draw for the prize.

## Entry Points System

| Purchase Amount | Entries |
|---|---|
| Rs 1,000/- | 5 Entries |
| Rs 2,000/- | 10 Entries |
| Rs 3,000/- | 15 Entries |
| Rs 5,000/- | 25 Entries |
| Rs 10,000/- | 50 Entries |

For every Rs. 1,000 spent, a customer receives 5 entries. Entries scale linearly with
spend (Rs 1,000 = 5 entries, so Rs 500 would round down proportionally — always quote
the table values directly for the amounts shown, and for other amounts explain the
Rs 1,000 = 5 entries rate rather than inventing an exact figure).

## Why Participate?

- Exclusive Ultra Rich rewards every month.
- More purchases unlock more chances to win.
- Automatic entry through qualifying purchases (no separate signup step required beyond
  purchasing).

## Answering customer questions about this program

- This is a genuine purchase-linked lucky-draw / loyalty rewards program, not a
  guaranteed-prize purchase — entries are chances in a monthly draw.
- If a customer asks "how do I enter" — the answer is simply: buy qualifying Ultra Rich
  product(s); entries are calculated automatically from the purchase value.
