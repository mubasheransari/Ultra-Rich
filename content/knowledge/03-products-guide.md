# Product Range — Buying Guide

Mezan Ultra Rich is sold in five product formats. Exact current sizes and prices for
each are provided as separate reference chunks generated directly from the product
catalog, so always defer to those for numbers — this file is about *which format suits
which use case*.

## Pouch

A resealable stand-up pouch with a carry handle. This is the best default choice for a
household that goes through tea regularly — it's available in the widest range of
sizes, so it scales from a small trial size up to a large family-size pack.

## Jar

Loose tea in a reusable plastic jar with a carrying handle and screw lid. Good for
kitchens that prefer scooping tea from a fixed countertop container rather than
resealing a bag, and the jar itself can be reused afterwards.

## Hard Pack

A compact, protective box (not a bag) that keeps the leaf tightly sealed. This is a good
grab-and-go or gifting size, and a good way to try the tea in a smaller quantity than the
pouch.

## Tea Bags

Individually sealed black tea bags. Best for offices, travel, or anyone who wants a
consistent, mess-free single serving without measuring loose leaf. Ready wherever the
day takes you.

## Green Tea Bags

Mint and lemon green tea variants, sold as tea bags. These are lighter, more aromatic,
and quietly indulgent compared to the black tea range — a good option for an afternoon
or evening cup, or for anyone who prefers green tea to black tea generally.

## General guidance for "which one should I buy" questions

- New to the brand and want to try it first: **Hard Pack** (smallest commitment) or the
  smallest available **Pouch** size.
- Regular household daily use: **Pouch** (largest sizes available) or **Jar**.
- Office / travel / minimal mess: **Tea Bags**.
- Want something lighter than black tea, or don't drink tea with milk: **Green Tea
  Bags** (mint or lemon).
- Gifting: **Hard Pack** or **Jar** tend to present best as a gift.
