# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where is Ultra Rich tea from?**
A: It's grown in a single estate in the Nandi Hills of Kenya's highlands.

**Q: What formats does Ultra Rich come in?**
A: Pouch, Jar, Hard Pack, Tea Bags, and Green Tea Bags (mint and lemon). See the
product-guide and per-product reference chunks for exact sizes and prices.

**Q: Is there a green tea option?**
A: Yes — Mezan Ultra Rich Green Tea Bags, available in mint and lemon variants.

**Q: Do you deliver nationwide?**
A: Yes, free delivery is offered nationwide on all orders placed through the website.

**Q: What if I'm not happy with my purchase?**
A: Ultra Rich offers a 7-day satisfaction promise / returns window. For the exact
return process, contact customer support at customersupport@mezangrp.com or
+92 337 1046238.

**Q: How do I win a prize through Ultra Rich World?**
A: Purchase qualifying Ultra Rich products — entries into the monthly draw are earned
automatically based on how much you spend (Rs 1,000 spent = 5 entries). See the
"Ultra Rich World" rewards content for the full entry table.

**Q: Is Ultra Rich available in my city?**
A: Check the "Where to Find Us" page's store locator (search by location or store
name). This knowledge base only has a sample list of stockists, so if a city isn't in
that sample, don't say it's unavailable — suggest checking the live store locator or
contacting customer support.

**Q: How do I apply for a job at Mezan?**
A: Email your CV to teacareers@mezangrp.com with the position title in the subject
line. See the "Grow With Us" careers content for current openings and requirements.

**Q: Does Ultra Rich sell coffee or other beverages?**
A: No — based on the available information, Ultra Rich is a tea brand (black tea and
green tea bags). Don't claim other product categories exist unless the retrieved
context confirms it.
