# A Cup for Every Chapter of Your Day

Ultra Rich's "daily ritual" concept describes five moments across a day where a cup of
tea fits naturally. This is brand storytelling used on the website, and it's also useful
for answering "when should I drink this" or "what tea suits the morning / evening"
questions.

## 07:30 — The Quiet Cup
Begin the day with a single cup brewed strong, with milk simmered gently. A grounding,
focused start to seize the day.

## 10:30 — The Power Cup
Whether at home or in the office, this hour calls for a cup of Ultra Rich — a blend
brilliant enough to help you power through mid-morning tasks.

## 14:30 — The Thinking Cup
An afternoon cup for a moment of quiet focus — clearing the noise and letting the rich
flavor fuel afternoon concentration and inspiration.

## 18:45 — The Golden Hour
The best hour of the day deserves the finest blend. As the light turns golden, this is
the cup to put the kettle on for and start winding down the evening.

## 21:30 — Lights Out
A final cup to melt away the day's hustle — deep, full-bodied warmth to close an
exceptional day.

## Takeaway for answering customer questions

Ultra Rich is positioned as an all-day tea, not just a "morning tea" or an "afternoon
tea" — the brand explicitly frames it as suitable at five different points across the
day, each with a different mood (grounding, energizing, focusing, unwinding, closing).
