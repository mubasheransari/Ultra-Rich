## Current Openings (Karachi)

### Social Media Executive — Karachi
- Employment Type: Permanent. Application deadline: June 25, 2026.
- Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Media Sciences, Communications,
  Business Administration, or a related field; 1–2 years of experience in social media
  management, community management, or customer engagement.
- Key skills: excellent written/verbal English communication; strong understanding of
  social platforms and community management; experience with Instagram, Facebook,
  TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube scheduling/publishing; ability to create reels/short-form
  video; basic Canva/Photoshop; photography and videography skills; awareness of digital
  trends.
- Key responsibilities: manage Mezan Tea's social communities; respond to comments,
  messages, reviews and queries; monitor sentiment and trends; plan engagement
  campaigns; coordinate with internal teams; track performance; manage online
  reputation.

### Commercial Finance Lead — Karachi
- Application deadline: 20 July 2026.
- Requirements: CA part qualified / ACCA / ICMA qualified; 3–5 years relevant
  experience; strong commercial acumen, communication, negotiation, analytical and
  strategic thinking skills.
- Key responsibilities: assist CFO on commercial strategy; manage pricing & margins;
  manage distributor commercials and ROI; monitor trade spend & ROI; manage commercial
  credit; budgeting/forecasting; business analytics and dashboards; cross-functional
  collaboration.

### Tax and Compliance Lead — Karachi
- Application deadline: 20 July 2026.
- Requirements: CA Part Qualified / ACCA / ICMA; 5–8 years relevant tax experience,
  preferably at a reputable taxation firm; experience representing independently before
  tax authorities; strong analytical, problem-solving, negotiation and communication
  skills.
- Key responsibilities: manage direct & indirect tax compliance; lead tax planning &
  risk management; manage tax audits & regulatory liaison; ensure correct withholding
  tax deduction and statutory compliance; manage tax refunds/exemptions/credits; develop
  tax policies, SOPs & internal controls; lead tax process automation.
