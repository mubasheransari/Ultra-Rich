# Born in the Highlands — Origin Story

Mezan Ultra Rich tea is grown in Kenya's celebrated highlands — specifically the Nandi
Hills, a single-estate growing region. In these highlands, nature patiently nurtures the
depth and character that make every cup of Ultra Rich distinctive: high altitude, rich
volcanic soil, and a cool, misty climate slow the growth of the tea plant (Camellia
sinensis), which concentrates flavor and color in the leaf.

When the leaves reach their finest expression, they're carefully selected and expertly
crafted to preserve everything the land has given them. What arrives in the cup is more
than tea — it's the result of a place, a process, and a commitment to creating
something that feels unmistakably Ultra Rich.

## Why Kenyan tea specifically

Kenya is one of the world's largest tea-exporting countries, and its highland black
teas are prized for:

- A brisk, full-bodied character with good color strength (important for the milky
  "doodh patti" style tea common in Pakistan and South Asia).
- Bright, coppery-to-reddish liquor color even at moderate brewing strength.
- Consistent quality due to CTC (Crush-Tear-Curl) processing, which is well suited to
  quick, strong infusions — ideal for tea brewed with milk.

## Single estate

"Single estate" means the tea in Ultra Rich comes from one defined growing area (Nandi
Hills) rather than being a blend sourced from many different origins. This is called out
on packaging and in marketing as a mark of quality and traceability.
